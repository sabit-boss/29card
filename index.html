📖 How to Play 29

  • 4 players in 2 teams (partners sit opposite)
  • 32-card deck (7 through Ace in each suit)
  • Each player gets 8 cards
  • Trump suit is revealed — highest trump or highest led-suit card wins the trick
  • You must follow the led suit if you can
  • Points: A=11, 10=10, K=4, Q=3, J=2, others=0
  • First team to reach 29 wins! 🏆
