📖 How to Play 29

  • 4 players in 2 teams (partners sit opposite)
  • 32 cards, ranked J > 9 > A > 10 > K > Q > 8 > 7
  • Each player gets 8 cards (4 before bidding, 4 after)
  • Bid 16–29 points — the points your team will take. Highest bidder declares trump.
  • You must follow the led suit if you can
  • Card points: J=3, 9=2, A=1, 10=1 — 28 in the deck, +1 for the last trick = 29
  • Make your bid → your team +1. Miss it → the other team +1.
  • First team to +6 wins the match! 🏆
